## What Changed

- Grouped AI input, calculate action, and live recognition into `assistantQueryPanel`.
- Removed the confusing play icon from `Berechnen`.
- Made `Verlauf` a quieter `utilityButton` and kept `KI öffnen/ausblenden` as the distinct panel action.
- Added dynamic UI state hooks for preview, AI status, and live query preview.
- Styled `neutral`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, and `error` states across themes.
- Preserved result-confirmed feedback after pressing `=`, instead of immediately overwriting it with live preview.
- Added regression tests for expanded panel structure, top-bar priority, and error recovery.
